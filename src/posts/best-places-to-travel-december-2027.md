A December trip can mean hot chocolate after a cold walk, a week on a Pacific beach, or a road trip during the Southern Hemisphere summer. Start by choosing the experience you want most. Vienna suits a short break built around festive streets and museums. Rovaniemi suits travelers who want Arctic winter and can accept very short daylight. Costa Rica or Phuket makes more sense when warmth is the priority. Cape Town and New Zealand reward travelers who have time to leave a city base and adjust their route around the weather.

This guide compares ten places for December 2027 using recurring seasonal conditions. The days suggested for each destination are planning estimates, and the weather describes typical patterns. Confirm exact 2027 event schedules, operating hours, and holiday transport with the operator before making a booking that is hard to change.

## 1. Vienna, Austria

Choose Vienna if you want a December city break where the evening is part of the itinerary. Put one market visit after dark and keep a museum or palace for the brightest hours of the following day. The city tourism board lists seasonal programming as it becomes available. Individual Christmas markets can have different closing dates, so verify the one you want to visit before assuming it will run through your full stay.

A practical three-night plan gives the first evening to the central streets, the second day to a museum and another neighborhood, and the final full day to Schönbrunn or a slower café and gallery itinerary. Choose accommodation with easy public transport if you want to combine several neighborhoods and return to a warm base between activities. Pack shoes that can handle wet paving, even if you are hoping for snow.

Travelers visiting around Christmas should keep flexible plans for their arrival and departure days. Reserve the meal, performance, or palace entry that would matter most to the trip. Leave more ordinary activities open until the weekend and holiday hours are confirmed. If a specific market is the deciding factor, a stay earlier in December gives you more room to compare the published dates before you book.

## 2. Rovaniemi, Finland

Visit Rovaniemi for Arctic darkness, winter activities, and the possibility of seeing the northern lights. The local tourism board places December inside its aurora season and lists very short days in the middle of winter. A clear night improves your chances, but an aurora outing still depends on activity in the sky, local cloud cover, and access to a sufficiently dark location.

Book the trip around something you would value in any weather. A visit to a museum and a planned snow activity can anchor the days, while keeping multiple evenings free gives you options for the aurora. Ask tour providers how they handle poor conditions and whether they supply thermal gear. Confirm what is included and how far you have to travel to the pickup point in cold weather.

Avoid putting an outdoor excursion immediately after a tight flight connection. If a delay pushes your arrival past dinner, a walk near the hotel or an indoor plan for the evening is easier to salvage. Visitors traveling with children should pay particular attention to warming stops, time outdoors, and the conditions in which an operator will shorten a trip.

## 3. Québec City, Canada

Québec City suits travelers who want a compact winter trip with historic streets, indoor stops, and the option of snow activities outside the center. Start in Old Québec for a walk through the upper and lower town. Build the route around short segments with places to warm up, especially if you are carrying camera gear or traveling with a group that walks at different speeds.

A four-night stay can leave one full day for the old city, one for museums and food, and another for a nearby winter activity if conditions permit. Check which seasonal activities are operating during your exact dates. A winter tourism page can confirm that a city supports an activity in season, while the operator's schedule and conditions determine whether you can do it that day.

Ice and slopes can change how quickly you move between neighborhoods. If stairs or steep streets are a concern, ask your hotel about the easiest route and identify stops where you can sit down indoors. Choose a central base if you want to make short outings with an easy return, and compare transport options before booking a property far outside the old city.

## 4. Tokyo, Japan

Tokyo is a strong December choice for travelers who care more about food, neighborhoods, and urban lights than about snow. Japan's national tourism guide highlights winter illuminations and the approach of the year-end holiday period. Pick one area for daytime sightseeing and one for an evening lights route. Grouping activities by neighborhood reduces time lost to unnecessary transfers.

For a five-night stay, spend one day around a museum or garden, one on food and shopping streets, and one in an area you can explore after dark. Keep a day flexible for a side trip or a second visit to a neighborhood you liked. If you are traveling with a group whose interests differ, selecting a meeting point near a major station can make a split itinerary easier to manage.

Around New Year, verify dates for museums, small businesses, and restaurants individually. A venue's usual weekly hours can differ from its year-end schedule. If you want to spend December 31 in Tokyo, decide where you will eat and how you will get back to your hotel before fixing the rest of the evening.

## 5. Tenerife, Spain

Choose Tenerife for a mild coastal base with a distinctly different landscape in the mountains. The island tourism board describes a mild coastal winter, and Spain's meteorological agency provides climate normals for Tenerife South Airport. Airport averages describe one specific place. A high-elevation day around Teide can bring colder conditions than a walk on the coast.

A workable week includes several coastal days, an inland outing, and a reserve day for your priority outdoor activity. If you are choosing a base for beach access, compare beaches and the ability to reach restaurants without a long drive. If your main goal is hiking, map your destinations before choosing a base. An inexpensive room can be a poor fit if the route to your daily activities is inconvenient.

Check the operating status of any mountain service near the date and pack layers that fit the elevation change. If the high country is unsuitable, use the day for towns or a coastal route and keep a later morning free. That flexibility lets you choose the mountain outing based on conditions rather than on a prepaid schedule.

## 6. Dubai, United Arab Emirates

Dubai fits a short break focused on outdoor dining, city sights, and easy switches between outdoor and indoor activities. Visit Dubai describes December as part of the cooler season. Plan outdoor walks or waterfront time during daylight and use the evening for the sights or meals you want most. A central location can make it easier to pair activities without crossing the city unnecessarily.

For a four-night stay, group older districts and creek-side sights in one outing, then use a separate day for the beach or a different part of the city. Compare opening hours and transport options before placing two timed reservations on the same afternoon. Travel times can matter more than the apparent distance on a map, particularly around busy events.

Verify the actual 2027 program for any December celebration that would influence your dates. At year-end, check pickup and drop-off arrangements and ask whether your reservation includes access to the area during road restrictions. A flexible city break is easier to manage if you commit to only one major fixed activity on a crowded night.

## 7. Phuket, Thailand

Phuket is a good fit if you want beaches and an easy inland fallback. Choose a beach base that matches how you like to spend evenings, then allow a separate morning or afternoon for Phuket Old Town. The Tourism Authority of Thailand's guide to the old town gives you a starting point for a walking route and food stops that do not require a boat booking.

Consider how often you actually want to be on the water. If a boat outing is the centerpiece of your trip, put it early enough that you can move it if conditions change. Check the operator's cancellation and transfer rules and pay attention to conditions at your specific beach, since a general seasonal description does not tell you whether swimming or boating is suitable on an individual day.

A six-night plan works well with one primary beach, an old-town day, one boat outing if it runs, and at least one day with no fixed booking. Changing hotels mid-trip costs part of a day, so weigh that move against the convenience of settling into one location.

## 8. Guanacaste, Costa Rica

Guanacaste is a useful December option for a Pacific coast trip with time for wildlife and slower days near the beach. Costa Rica's official tourism guidance places the Pacific dry season generally from December to April, with conditions varying by region and year. December arrives near the seasonal transition, so make room for rain and check the forecast near your dates.

Choose one beach base and one nature activity you can reach without turning the trip into a series of transfers. A beach day, a guided outing, and a spare day can make a more coherent trip than trying to pair the far ends of the country in one week. Before committing to an inland stop, check how long it takes on the route you will actually use.

Ask your accommodation or operator what road conditions to expect and how pickups work. If you plan to drive, allow daylight after landing where possible. Holiday availability varies by property and service, so check the dates of the rooms and transfers together before paying for either one.

## 9. Cape Town, South Africa

Cape Town offers a Southern Hemisphere summer with a choice of mountain, coast, garden, and city days. The local tourism board describes December as summer and notes that strong wind can be part of the season. That makes it worth scheduling several possible windows for exposed activities. Pick one mountain or coastal outing you particularly care about and keep another day available if it moves.

For a six-night itinerary, plan a city day, a waterfront or garden day, and a longer outing toward the peninsula if you want to leave town. Keep the itinerary within a manageable area each day. If the weather closes a high or exposed attraction, move your indoor or sheltered activity forward rather than trying to replace it with another outing vulnerable to the same wind.

Beach preferences matter here. Some people mainly want an afternoon walk and a view, while others need water conditions that suit swimming. Check local beach information near your travel dates and avoid making the choice from the air temperature alone. A summer day on land can still feel quite different in the water or on an exposed ridge.

## 10. Queenstown, New Zealand

Queenstown suits a December trip built around lake and mountain scenery and longer daylight. Tourism New Zealand describes December as the start of summer, while also emphasizing changeable conditions. The extra light gives you room to move some sightseeing around the day. It does not make alpine routes predictable or reduce the time you need to drive between towns.

Give a week-long trip one main base, time on the lakefront, and a small number of longer outings. If you want to see another part of the South Island, choose it on the basis of actual driving time and how often you would need to pack up. A map may make a long detour look like a convenient day trip, but rest stops and weather can add time. Keep an uncommitted day if there is a flight, boat, or guided walk you especially want to do.

Check access and trail conditions with the operator or land manager before heading into the mountains. Choose one shorter route and one town-based fallback for any high-country day. Travelers who build around those alternatives can still use the long daylight without committing to an exposed itinerary regardless of conditions.

## How to choose your December 2027 trip

For a short festive break, compare Vienna and Québec City by flight time, walking conditions, and holiday hours. For warmer days, compare Tenerife and Dubai against longer stays in Phuket or Guanacaste. If the trip is chiefly about nature, choose whether you want Arctic winter in Rovaniemi or summer scenery in Cape Town and Queenstown. Tokyo works when the city experience matters more than a specific climate or outdoor activity.

Once you have two candidates, pick a fixed date range, confirm the single activity that justifies the trip, and check the holiday schedule for that activity and your transport. If either destination has several workable alternatives for a bad-weather day or a closure, that flexibility is worth weighing alongside the flight and accommodation price.
