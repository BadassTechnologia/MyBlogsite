Breaking into tech — or leveling up once you're in — gets a lot easier when you're following a clear roadmap instead of guessing what to learn next. This post rounds up where to find solid, up-to-date roadmaps and where to actually find the jobs once you're ready.

## Roadmap resources

Sites like roadmap.sh lay out step-by-step learning paths for roles like frontend developer, backend developer, DevOps engineer, and data analyst — including what to learn first, what's optional, and what's outdated. They're community-maintained, so they tend to stay current with what employers are actually asking for.

## Where to find the postings

Once you know what role you're aiming for, general boards (LinkedIn, Indeed) work fine for breadth, but niche boards — like We Work Remotely, Wellfound (formerly AngelList), and company career pages directly — often surface roles days before they hit the big aggregators.
