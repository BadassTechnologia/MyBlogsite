A domain name is more than a website address. It represents your brand, helps users find your website, and can communicate what your project does.

But what is the difference between `.com`, `.org`, `.io`, `.ai`, and `.dev`? Which extension should you choose for a startup, portfolio, API, or online store?

This guide explains the most common domain extensions, their purposes, and the practical details to check before buying one.

## 1. Understanding domain names

Consider this URL:

```text
https://www.example.com
```

Its components are:

- `https`: The communication protocol.
- `www`: A hostname, commonly used as a subdomain.
- `example`: The registered domain name.
- `.com`: The top-level domain (TLD).

A domain name can also contain subdomains:

```text
example.com
blog.example.com
api.example.com
shop.example.com
```

The Internet Assigned Numbers Authority (IANA) maintains the [DNS root zone database](https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db), which lists officially delegated top-level domains.

## 2. Traditional domain extensions

Generic top-level domains (gTLDs) are not tied to a specific country. Some of the oldest and most recognizable extensions include:

| Extension | Original purpose | Common usage |
|---|---|---|
| `.com` | Commercial | Businesses, startups, personal websites |
| `.org` | Organizations | Nonprofits and communities |
| `.net` | Network-related | Technology and infrastructure |
| `.info` | Information | Informational websites |
| `.biz` | Business | Commercial websites |

Today, most of these extensions are available for a wide range of websites, subject to registry rules.

## 3. Country-code domain extensions

Country-code top-level domains (ccTLDs) represent specific countries or territories.

| Extension | Country or territory |
|---|---|
| `.in` | India |
| `.us` | United States |
| `.uk` | United Kingdom |
| `.ca` | Canada |
| `.au` | Australia |
| `.de` | Germany |
| `.fr` | France |
| `.cn` | China |
| `.jp` | Japan |
| `.br` | Brazil |

Some country-code extensions have also become popular internationally:

| Extension | Origin | Popular usage |
|---|---|---|
| `.io` | British Indian Ocean Territory | Technology and startups |
| `.ai` | Anguilla | Artificial intelligence |
| `.me` | Montenegro | Personal websites |
| `.tv` | Tuvalu | Video and media |
| `.gg` | Guernsey | Gaming and esports |
| `.co` | Colombia | Companies and startups |

Google treats some country-code extensions, including `.ai`, `.io`, `.tv`, and `.me`, as generic domains for search geotargeting. However, most ccTLDs still provide a country-specific signal. See [Google Search Central's guidance for multi-regional websites](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites).

## 4. Restricted and sponsored extensions

Some domain extensions have eligibility requirements.

| Extension | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `.gov` | US government entities |
| `.edu` | Eligible US educational institutions |
| `.mil` | US military |
| `.int` | Certain international treaty-based organizations |

Other countries have their own government and education domain structures. For example, India uses extensions such as `.gov.in`, `.ac.in`, and `.edu.in`, subject to their respective registration policies.

## 5. Modern domain extensions

Newer domain extensions let websites communicate their purpose through their address.

| Extension | Common usage |
|---|---|
| `.app` | Applications and mobile products |
| `.dev` | Developers and software projects |
| `.tech` | Technology companies |
| `.cloud` | Cloud platforms |
| `.studio` | Creative studios |
| `.shop` | Online shopping |
| `.store` | E-commerce |
| `.software` | Software products |
| `.design` | Designers and creative portfolios |
| `.online` | General online businesses |

For developers, `.dev` and `.app` are particularly useful. Both are included in browser HSTS preload lists, so websites using them must support HTTPS.

### What is coming in 2027?

ICANN's 2026 New gTLD Program has 1,610 applications proceeding to evaluation. The next stages include public input, objections, and technical evaluation. The prioritization draw is expected in the first half of 2027.

This could eventually introduce more domain extensions, but an application does not mean the extension is already available for public registration. Read [ICANN's String Confirmation Day announcement](https://www.icann.org/en/announcements/details/icann-confirms-2026-round-applications-input-and-objections-period-begins-17-11-2026-en) and follow the [ICANN New gTLD Program](https://newgtldprogram.icann.org/) for current information.

## 6. Reserved domain extensions

Not every domain extension can be registered. Some names are reserved for testing, documentation, or internal networking.

| Extension | Intended purpose |
|---|---|
| `.localhost` | Local computer testing |
| `.test` | DNS testing |
| `.example` | Documentation and examples |
| `.invalid` | Intentionally invalid domain names |

These extensions should not be treated as normal public domain registrations. They are defined in [RFC 6761](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6761).

## 8. What is the difference between `www` and `https`?

These two terms serve completely different purposes.

Consider these URLs:

```text
https://example.com
https://www.example.com
http://example.com
```

### What is `www`?

`www` stands for World Wide Web. It is a hostname, commonly configured as a subdomain.

- `example.com`: Root domain.
- `www.example.com`: WWW subdomain.
- `blog.example.com`: Blog subdomain.
- `api.example.com`: API subdomain.

You do not need `www` to run a website. Both `example.com` and `www.example.com` can point to the same website.

### What is HTTPS?

HTTPS stands for Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure. It uses TLS encryption to protect communication between a browser and a website.

| Feature | WWW | HTTPS |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning | World Wide Web | Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure |
| Role | Hostname | Communication protocol |
| Purpose | Identifies a website host | Encrypts data in transit |
| Required? | No | Strongly recommended |

Both of these are valid:

```text
https://example.com
https://www.example.com
```

There is no universal SEO advantage to choosing one over the other. Choose one as your primary URL and permanently redirect the other to it using an HTTP 301 redirect. Also ensure your SSL/TLS certificate covers both hostnames.

Use HTTPS everywhere, including login pages, APIs, and websites that do not collect sensitive information. For additional background, see [MDN's explanation of HTTPS](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Glossary/HTTPS).

## 9. Which domain extension should you choose?

Your choice should depend on your project and audience.

| Project | Suggested extension |
|---|---|
| Global business | `.com` |
| Indian business | `.in` or `.com` |
| AI startup | `.ai` or `.com` |
| Developer portfolio | `.dev` or `.me` |
| SaaS product | `.com`, `.app`, or `.io` |
| Open-source community | `.org` |
| Online store | `.shop`, `.store`, or `.com` |
| API or technology platform | `.com`, `.io`, or `.dev` |

A short, memorable domain is usually more valuable than an unusual extension. Choose something easy to spell, pronounce, and remember.

## 10. Things to check before buying

Before registering a domain:

1. **Renewal price:** Check the cost after the first year.
2. **Ownership:** Register it under your own account and email.
3. **Privacy:** Check whether WHOIS privacy is included.
4. **Security:** Enable two-factor authentication and domain locking.
5. **Trademark:** Make sure the name does not infringe on another brand.
6. **DNS management:** Ensure you can configure A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, and TXT records.
7. **HTTPS:** Confirm that your hosting provider supports SSL/TLS.

Buying a domain does not automatically give you website hosting. A domain is your web address; hosting is where your website's files and application run.

## Conclusion

Domain extensions help communicate what a website represents, but they do not determine its success.

Whether you choose `.com`, `.in`, `.ai`, `.io`, or `.dev`, focus on a memorable name, reliable registration, transparent renewal pricing, and proper security.

For most businesses, `.com` remains a strong default. For developers and startups, newer extensions can offer more specific branding. Choose the domain that fits your project, not just the trend.
