A marketing team that already has reliable sales records may need a better way to hand off qualified leads. An ecommerce team may need timely welcome, abandoned checkout, and post-purchase messages. A product-led business may care most about identifying a user who signed up but never completed setup. These teams can all buy marketing automation software, but a convincing demonstration for one can be irrelevant to the others.

Select your first platform by the event, the audience, and the outcome it needs to support. For CRM-centered inbound marketing, compare HubSpot and the platform that fits your current CRM. For ecommerce, evaluate Klaviyo and Omnisend with a copy of your expected store events. For product-event messaging, look at Customer.io. An app connector such as Zapier can help with handoffs across systems when you already have a dependable consent and messaging platform.

## 1. HubSpot Marketing Hub

[HubSpot Marketing Hub](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/marketing) suits teams that want contact history, campaigns, and sales follow-up connected to one CRM. Test it with a download or demo request. Check whether the form creates or updates the correct record, whether the team can see the contact's response history, and whether the workflow stops when sales has already taken ownership. If several business units share a database, confirm who can view or modify each audience and asset.

**Pricing**. HubSpot offers free tools and displayed Starter from $7 per month. Starter is priced per seat. The page displayed Professional at $800 per month and describes onboarding charges for paid higher tiers. Check whether the rate is promotional, which contract applies, and what additional contacts or seats would cost.

## 2. ActiveCampaign

[ActiveCampaign](https://www.activecampaign.com/platform/marketing-automation) is useful when an email program needs to react to user behavior and run more than one path through a journey. Build a sample workflow with a branch for people who complete an action and a separate path for people who do not. Check which conditions and actions are available on the exact tier you are testing. A simplified plan can be suitable for straightforward campaigns while limiting a team that needs several decision points or more advanced reporting.

**Pricing**. The official help center documents Starter and higher plans. The public pricing page could not be verified for a numeric starting rate during the review. Size your estimate with the vendor using your contacts, feature set, and billing term.

[Image suggestion: Screenshot of the official ActiveCampaign marketing automation landing page.]

## 3. Brevo

[Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/pricing/) is worth testing when campaign and transactional volume are central to the buying decision. To compare it with a contact-based plan, forecast a quiet month and a peak month from actual sends, and check which program will produce each message. Keep account notices apart from optional marketing in the test so that consent and suppression behavior can be inspected where each message originates.

**Pricing**. Brevo's official help center shows a free plan, Starter from $9 per month, and Standard from $18 per month. Contact storage, message volume, SMS credits, and automation capabilities can affect which plan fits.

[Image suggestion: Screenshot of the official Brevo marketing platform landing page.]

## 4. Mailchimp

[Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/pricing/marketing/) is a good candidate for a small team that wants to combine newsletters and simple automated sequences. An easy pilot is a welcome email followed by a message if the recipient takes a particular action. Let the person who will maintain the workflow set up the pilot so you can see which tasks are easy to repeat and where that person needs outside help. Test how an unsubscribed user appears in the audience and what happens when someone imports an old record.

**Pricing**. The official page showed free access for up to 250 contacts and an Essentials offer displayed at $13 per month for 12 months. The contact tier, send limits, promotion eligibility, and renewal terms can change your cost. Check which flow features the chosen plan includes.

[Image suggestion: Screenshot of the official Mailchimp marketing platform landing page.]

## 5. Klaviyo

[Klaviyo](https://www.klaviyo.com/pricing) is a candidate for retailers using purchase or browsing activity to trigger relevant messages. Test a welcome flow, a cart-related flow, and a post-purchase flow that depends on product category. Check whether the expected event reaches Klaviyo with the same customer identity used for consent and ownership. A store integration that imports orders but cannot pass an important identifier may make it difficult to distinguish repeat buyers from visitors who check out under different addresses.

**Pricing**. Klaviyo lists a free plan and an Email plan from $20 per month. Profile volume and channel use can change the price. The official page also displayed a temporary Marketing offer, so use the normal term and expected profile count when predicting renewal cost.

[Image suggestion: Screenshot of the official Klaviyo landing page.]

## 6. Customer.io

[Customer.io](https://customer.io/pricing) fits product or growth teams that already collect reliable user events and want those events to control journeys. An onboarding workflow could send a reminder to people who started setup but did not finish it, and stop sending as soon as the product records completion. Define the event and the sending window before building your first workflow. Keep a test account where you can simulate repeated events, delayed delivery, and a user who opts out in the middle of the journey.

**Pricing**. Essentials starts at $100 per month on the official page. Profiles, message volume, channels, and the feature level are inputs to confirm. Budget for engineering time if the product needs new instrumentation.

[Image suggestion: Screenshot of the official Customer.io landing page.]

## 7. Salesforce Marketing Cloud Account Engagement

[Salesforce Marketing Cloud Account Engagement](https://www.salesforce.com/marketing/b2b-automation/pricing/) fits B2B teams that use Salesforce and want campaigns linked to sales records and lead qualification. Test how one attendee moves from an event registration into an active lead or contact. Agree with sales on who changes lifecycle stages and what happens if a salesperson already owns the account. Then check how the platform reports a marketing interaction alongside related opportunities. Before comparing products, keep that definition of attribution consistent.

**Pricing**. Growth+ is listed at $1,250 per organization per month billed annually. Salesforce notes that per-month products on its pricing page require an annual contract. Review contact capacity, additional modules, and implementation costs against your current Salesforce setup.

[Image suggestion: Screenshot of the official Salesforce Account Engagement landing page.]

## 8. Adobe Marketo Engage

[Adobe Marketo Engage](https://business.adobe.com/products/marketo/pricing.html) suits enterprise B2B teams evaluating marketing programs with complex journeys and operational ownership. Bring a representative program to the demo, such as a webinar followed by region-specific follow-up and a documented lead handoff. Ask to see how a team can reuse the program without inadvertently changing another region's assets or sending to the wrong audience. Include the people who will maintain templates and data integrations in the evaluation.

**Pricing**. Adobe describes multiple packages and directs prospective buyers to sales for pricing. Request a quote that includes the capabilities you need, the expected database size, and the support or implementation effort required to launch.

[Image suggestion: Screenshot of the official Adobe Marketo Engage landing page.]

## 9. Omnisend

[Omnisend](https://www.omnisend.com/pricing/) is designed for merchants who want to build recurring ecommerce campaigns across email and SMS. Compare it with Klaviyo using the same store and customer scenarios so both tools receive the same order and cart data. Ask the people who will work in it to build a usable email and verify that a test order reaches the intended segment. Confirm SMS eligibility and country-specific rates before estimating a multichannel campaign's cost.

**Pricing**. Omnisend lists a free tier and paid plans in a contact-based calculator. The visible rate depended on the selected billing period and promotional settings. Enter your actual contact count and review the renewal price and any separate SMS charges.

[Image suggestion: Screenshot of the official Omnisend landing page.]

## 10. Zapier

[Zapier](https://zapier.com/pricing) helps when a marketing program needs to pass information between apps that your team already uses. A team could route event registrations to a CRM and notify the correct account owner, while leaving sends and consent in the established marketing platform. Test a webhook or form that sends the same registration twice and a downstream app that temporarily fails. The goal is to see how the handoff behaves under ordinary retries, rather than only when every connected system responds immediately.

**Pricing**. Zapier lists a free tier and shows Professional from $19.99 per month on annual billing. Check the task allowance, the features required for your integration, and how retries and multi-step flows affect consumption before using that figure for your projected volume.

## Run the same pilot before comparing quotes

Choose one high-value journey and record the event that starts it, the audience entitled to receive it, the source of consent, and the condition that stops it. For example, a product trial reminder could start after signup, wait until the next day, exclude anyone who finished setup, and route all questionable records to a test group. Use the same sample data and expected outcomes across vendors. Include duplicate events, existing customers, and a contact who unsubscribes after entering the workflow.

Then calculate the first year and a steady-state quarter. Include the plan that actually passed the pilot, expected contacts or tasks, additional channel costs, onboarding, and the people who will maintain it. If your expected audience will cross a plan threshold near the busiest period, get that cost in writing before comparing the options. Give each platform a score for data reliability, the time it takes to build one working journey, how easily an operator can audit it, and the expected cost at realistic volume.

Finally, decide who owns each transition between systems. If sales receives a lead, define how the sales record acknowledges the handoff. If a user changes consent, define how long the campaign platform may take to learn about it. The price table helps build a shortlist. A working pilot shows which tool lets your team operate the journey under the conditions it will face after purchase.

## Compare outcomes after the pilot

Choose one business outcome and one operational measure to review after launch. For onboarding, track how many eligible users finish setup within seven days and how many messages the workflow sends per eligible user. Keep a holdout or a prior comparison period where that design makes sense. An increase in message volume can make the program look busier while producing little improvement in adoption. Give the operations lead access to the delivery and suppression logs so that differences can be traced to eligibility, consent, or system errors. Review those results before automating a second journey. The first rollout can reveal missing events or handoffs that would otherwise recur in every campaign.
